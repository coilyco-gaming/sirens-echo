  "reply": "A tidal lock is a state where a body's rotation rate stops changing over a full orbit, so it turns once per orbit and keeps one face pointed at its partner. The Moon is the familiar case: the same side of it always faces Earth. Tides raised by the partner's gravity drain the spin until it settles there. Locking usually takes the smaller body, though near-equal pairs like Pluto and Charon lock to each other.
