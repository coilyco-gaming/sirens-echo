  "reply": "Tidal locking is when a body's rotation settles so it always shows the same face to its partner — spin and orbit fall into step. Tides raised by the partner drag on the spin until the rate stops changing. Earth's Moon is locked to us, which is why its far side stays hidden. Pluto and Charon are locked to each other.
