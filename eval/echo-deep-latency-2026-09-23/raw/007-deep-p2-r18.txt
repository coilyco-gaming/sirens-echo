  "reply": "A tidal lock is when a body's rotation and its orbit fall into step, so the same face always points at the thing it orbits. The Moon is the familiar case: the same side faces Earth every day. It happens because the partner's gravity raises a bulge that drags on the spin, slowing it until rotation and orbit match. That state is called synchronous rotation.
