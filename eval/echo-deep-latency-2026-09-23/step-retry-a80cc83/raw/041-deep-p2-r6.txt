  "reply": "Tidal locking is when a body's rotation period matches its orbital period, so the same hemisphere always faces its partner. Earth's Moon is the familiar case: it turns once per orbit, which is why we only ever see one side. The cause is tidal friction. The larger body's gravity raises a bulge in the smaller one, and that drag bleeds off spin energy until rotation and orbit synchronise.
