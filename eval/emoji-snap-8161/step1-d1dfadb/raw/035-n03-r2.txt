You also get leather from animals, which is useful for crafting later.\n\n**Farming** is a close second if you want to focus on food production from the start. **Mining** is also viable if you want stone and basic tools early.\n\nThe choice really depends on what you want to do in your first few days — whether that's gathering food, building tools, or preparing for threats."
