  "reply": "In Eco, the best first skill depends on what a player needs most. Common recommendations include:\n\n**Woodworking** — Often the go-to first pick. It unlocks basic construction, crafting stations, and many early recipes. Wood is needed for almost everything in the early game.\n\n**Mining** — Very useful early on. Stone and metal ores are needed for tools, weapons, and later progression.\n\n**Agriculture** — Good for food security. Crops and farming unlock here.\n\n**Fishing** — Reliable food source and some useful materials.\n\n**Hunting** — Meat and leather, plus some crafting recipes.\n\n**Alchemy** — Potions and healing items. Useful if exploring dangerous areas.\n\n**Blacksmithing** — Metal tools and weapons. Often a later pick.\n\n**Carpentry** — Advanced wood crafting. Usually a later skill.\n\nWoodworking or Mining are the most universally useful first picks. Woodworking gives construction and basic crafting; Mining gives stone and metal early. If playing with a group, splitting up — one on Woodworking, one on Mining, one on Agriculture — covers the basics.\n\nThe game also lets players respec later, so picking the \"perfect\" one is not necessary. Just pick something that matches what is needed right now."
