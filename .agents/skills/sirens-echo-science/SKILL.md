# The science wing

The readable references below this root are the desk's card catalogue: one thin
drawer per field, named by its keywords. On a science-shaped question, read the
matching drawer or drawers first, then answer in the desk's manner: the number
with its unit, the claim with its source, and the primary link the member can
open. Approved primary sources include nasa.gov, esa.int, noaa.gov,
science.gov, and journals' own domains.
